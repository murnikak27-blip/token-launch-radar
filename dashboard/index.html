Token Launch Radar

Protocols with no token yet, priced as if they launched on today's record — and how to farm each one before they do.

How the implied value is derived

implied value = TVL × peer multiple × momentum × tokenless age × chain reach

The peer multiple is the median market-cap / TVL ratio of protocols in the same category whose token already trades, shrunk toward the global median when a category has few tokenized peers, with single ratios winsorized so one micro-cap cannot price a category. Momentum reads the seven-day TVL change, tokenless age rewards a long record at size with no token, and chain reach counts deployments. A versioned child of a tokenized parent (an Aave V3 beside a trading AAVE) is discounted to 0.35×: its value accrues to the parent's token. Custodial records — bridges, canonical bridges, liquid staking and exchange balances — are left off the board: their TVL is other people's assets in custody.
